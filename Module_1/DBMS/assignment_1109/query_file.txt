1.  Display ename, sal, deptno, dname of all grade 3 salesman.

select e.ename, e.sal , d.deptno, d.dname, s.grade
from emp e
join dept d
on e.deptno = d.deptno
join salgrade s
on e.sal between s.losal and s.hisal
where s.grade = 3;

from emp e
join dept d
on e.deptno = d.deptno

					from dept d1 
					where d1.loc='dallas');

9. Company decides to give rise to all employees depending on their salary grade. Information of which is stored in increment table.

Create the table. Store  the data and solve the query.
Create  table increment
(
 grade int,
 hike float(5,2)
);

insert into increment values
(1,10),(2,7),(3,5),(4,2),(5,null);

Salary hike is in percentage.
Display empno, ename, deptno, dname, sal, grade ,hike  and new salary for all employees. New salary = salary + hike

select e.empno, e.ename, d.deptno, d.dname, e.sal, s.grade , i.hike , 
e.sal+((e.sal * (ifnull(i.hike, 0))) / 100) "new salary"
from emp e
join dept d
on e.deptno = d.deptno
join salgrade s
on e.sal between s.losal and s.hisal
join increment i
on s.grade = i.grade;
